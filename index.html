How the customer voice has moved month to month, store-wide. Complaints use complete data for the full year. Praise uses 4-star reviews, the only positive slice retrievable for 12 months, shown in the same monthly format so the two are comparable. On every chart, ignore the far-right tail: those months are still collecting reviews.

Complaints per month complete data

Complaint volume has drifted down over the year, roughly 100 to 145 a month early on toward 70 to 90 lately, with a holiday-season bump into December and January.

What the complaints are about % of each month's negatives

The "slow" and "too expensive" gripes have faded, while "never arrived" and "is it genuine" stay stubbornly flat. The pain is shifting from annoyance toward broken trust.

Praise per month 4-star reviews

Positive volume, in the same monthly format as complaints. It holds around 70 to 110 a month through early 2026, then falls off. That tail is mostly review lag: lukewarm 4-star reviews come in slowest, so recent months are still filling in, not a real drop in happy customers.

What the praise is about % of each month's 4-star

Fast delivery, quality and service as a share of that month's 4-star reviews. None of them climbs or falls clearly: the praise mix is broadly flat all year. Positive perception is steady, so the real movement is on the complaint side, not here.

What customers praise right now Jun to Sep 2026

The current snapshot, where full 4 and 5-star data exists: quality and fast delivery are neck and neck on top, far ahead of the rest. The strong fast-delivery praise mirrors the falling shipping complaints above.

Why praise uses 4-star reviews. Every public route was tested (date filters, deep paging, cache-busting, review-type slices) and Judge.me caps retrievable 5-star reviews at about four months. 4-star reviews are the only positive slice available for the full year, so the monthly praise trend is built from them. The full 5-star praise history older than four months is not exposed publicly and would need a Judge.me account export.
